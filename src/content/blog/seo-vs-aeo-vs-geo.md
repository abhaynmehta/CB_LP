Three acronyms, one goal: when someone looks for what you offer, they find you. The difference is where they look.

## SEO: be found in search results

**Search engine optimisation** makes a page easy for Google and Bing to crawl, understand and rank. The basics have not changed: a fast site, clear titles and descriptions, one page per topic, links between related pages, and content that answers the search better than the alternatives.

## AEO: be the answer that gets quoted

**Answer engine optimisation** shapes the page so its answer can be lifted out and quoted on its own, in a featured snippet, a voice answer or an AI summary. In practice that means:

- the answer in the first two or three sentences, before the detail
- headings phrased the way people ask questions
- short, self-contained paragraphs, tables for numbers, and a few common questions with direct answers
- FAQ and article structured data so machines can read the page's parts

Every post on this site opens with an "In short" paragraph for exactly this reason.

## GEO: be cited by AI tools

**Generative engine optimisation** is about ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity choosing to mention and link you. They favour sources that are:

- **crawlable by their bots**, which many sites block without knowing
- **specific**, with first-hand numbers they cannot find elsewhere
- **consistent**, telling the same story about who you are on your site, LinkedIn and other profiles
- **structured**, with clear entities: who the person or business is, where, and what they do

## How they fit together

| | SEO | AEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where | Google and Bing results | Snippets, voice and AI summaries | ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity |
| Goal | Rank | Be quoted | Be cited and recommended |
| Key work | Speed, structure, links, content | Answer-first writing, FAQs, schema | Bot access, original data, consistent profiles |
| Measure | Rankings, organic clicks | Snippets won, impressions | AI referrals, mentions in answers |

## What it did across 16 websites

At my day job I look after SEO, AEO and GEO for 16 real estate websites. In one month of working on all three:

| Measure | Change in one month |
|---|---|
| Organic leads | +19.3% |
| Branded clicks | +33.4% |
| ChatGPT referrals | +18% |
| Organic leads that qualify | up to 45% |

The last row matters most. Organic visitors who found a clear answer arrive better informed, and up to 45% of organic leads qualified.
