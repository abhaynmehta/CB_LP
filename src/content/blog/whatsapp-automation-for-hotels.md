Most resort enquiries arrive at the worst time: late at night, on weekends, in the middle of a wedding season rush. A guest who messages and waits until tomorrow for a reply has usually booked somewhere else by then.

Between August 2025 and February 2026 I worked as paid media and automation consultant for Machan Corbett Resort and Earavat Resort in Jim Corbett. The WhatsApp concierge was the part that turned ad clicks into bookings.

## What the concierge does

1. **Replies instantly**, at any hour, so the guest never waits.
2. **Sends the right brochure.** A couple planning a weekend gets a different brochure from a family planning a wedding or an HR team planning an offsite.
3. **Asks a few questions** that decide the quote: dates, number of guests and the kind of trip.
4. **Hands serious guests to the team** with the answers already collected, so the first human message is a quote, not a questionnaire.

## Different trips, different paths

The resorts sell very different things, so each one had its own campaign and its own follow-up:

| Revenue stream | Typical value |
|---|---|
| Destination weddings | ₹18 Lakh average ticket |
| Corporate retreats | ₹6 Lakh average group booking |
| Wildlife packages | Sold out the quarterly allocation |
| Leisure stays | The everyday base |

A wedding enquiry needs a planner's attention quickly. A weekend stay can be closed almost entirely on WhatsApp.

## The results

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp chat to booking | 22% (benchmark 4–8%) |
| Direct booking share | 18% → 41% |
| Confirmed direct bookings | ₹1.4 Crore+ |
| Ad spend | ₹1,20,000 (116x return) |

The full story of the campaigns is in [from 18% to 41% direct bookings](/blog/resort-direct-bookings-whatsapp/).

## What makes it work

- **Speed beats scripts.** The instant reply matters more than clever wording.
- **One brochure per intent.** Guests should only read about what they asked for.
- **Fewer questions.** Every extra question loses people; ask only what the quote needs.
- **A human for the big tickets.** Weddings and retreats close with a person, and the bot's job is to make that conversation fast.
