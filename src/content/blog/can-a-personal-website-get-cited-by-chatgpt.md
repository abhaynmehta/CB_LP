At my day job I look after SEO, AEO and GEO for 16 real estate websites. In one month that work lifted organic leads by 19.3%, branded clicks by 33.4% and ChatGPT referrals by 18%. Those are established brand sites with history behind them. I wanted to know something different: **can one person's portfolio, on a free subdomain with no history, get found by Google and cited by AI assistants?**

So I'm running the experiment in public, on this website. This is week 1.

## The three words, in plain English

- **SEO** (search engine optimisation): getting a page to rank in Google's normal results.
- **AEO** (answer engine optimisation): writing so a page can be quoted as a direct answer, in a featured snippet or a voice answer.
- **GEO** (generative engine optimisation): making it likely that AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews cite the page.

They are not separate jobs. A fast, clear, honest page with real information helps all three.

## What I did in week 1

| Step | What it is | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Phone score from 55 to 93–98 on Lighthouse | Slow pages lose visitors and rank worse |
| Sitemap | A list of every page on the site | Tells search engines what exists |
| robots.txt | Rules for crawlers | Lets Google, Bing and AI crawlers in |
| Canonical tags | Each page names its one true address | Avoids duplicate versions competing |
| Structured data | Labels search engines read: person, video, questions | Tells machines who I am and what each page is |
| Plain answers | Six short Q&As on the home page | The kind of passage AI tools quote |
| llms.txt | A plain-text summary for AI tools | A cheap extra for non-Google assistants |
| IndexNow | An instant "this page exists" ping to Bing | ChatGPT's search draws heavily on Bing |
| Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools | Verified ownership, submitted the sitemap | Lets me see indexing and searches |
| LinkedIn | Linked my profile to the site | Confirms the site and the profile are the same person |

## The hypotheses

1. **Bing indexes the site within days**, because I submitted it directly.
2. **Google indexes it within one to four weeks**, then shows it for searches that include my name and role.
3. **ChatGPT and Perplexity cite it** for questions about my specific work once Bing has it, because the pages contain numbers that exist nowhere else.
4. **Original numbers beat general advice.** Posts like the [real cost of a travel lead on Meta](/blog/meta-ads-cost-per-lead-travel-india/) carry data no one else publishes, which is exactly what AI tools look for when they cite.

## How I'll measure it

- **Google Search Console**: pages indexed, searches shown for, clicks.
- **Bing Webmaster Tools**: the same for Bing.
- **Prompts, run monthly**, three times each on ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google:
  - "Who is Abhay Mehta, digital marketing lead?"
  - "Digital marketing lead in Delhi NCR for real estate"
  - "What does a travel lead cost on Meta Ads in India?"
  - "How can a resort get more direct bookings than OTAs?"
  - "Real estate WhatsApp lead qualification case study"

AI answers change from one run to the next, so I'll report how often I'm cited (for example "2 of 3 runs"), not a single screenshot.
