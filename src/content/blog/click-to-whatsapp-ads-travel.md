Selling a Bali or Maldives trip is a conversation. People want to know what the package includes, whether the dates work and what it costs for their group, and they want to ask before they hand over a phone number.

From 2024 to 2026 I ran Meta Ads for 12+ tour operators selling international trips to Bali, the Maldives, Mauritius, Thailand, Vietnam and Dubai, generating 10,500+ trip enquiries. One click to WhatsApp campaign brought **10,694 chats at ₹4.62 each**.

## Chat ads vs lead forms

| | Click to WhatsApp | Lead form |
|---|---|---|
| Best result | 10,694 chats at ₹4.62 | 1,471 Bali leads at ₹50; 3,853 leads at ₹36.67 |
| Who starts | The traveller | The business calls back |
| Suits | Questions, itineraries, quick quotes | Teams working from a call list |
| Risk | Chats that go unanswered | Leads who forget they enquired |

The numbers are not directly comparable, because a chat is a conversation and a lead is a contact, but the pattern held across operators: chats cost far less and start warmer.

## What makes chat ads work

- **Answer within minutes.** A chat that waits until morning goes cold. Saved replies with the itinerary and price range help.
- **One destination per ad.** "Bali, 5 nights" brings people who want Bali, not people browsing.
- **Show the price.** A starting price in the creative filters out people the package does not suit.
- **Reels and statics both.** I made the creatives in house, in feed and reel formats; reels drove reach and statics with clear prices drove chats.

## Where lead forms still win

When the operator's team works from a call list, or wants to call group bookings directly, lead forms fit better. Group trips ran at ₹37 per lead and 1,025 Vietnam leads at ₹44.87. Most operators did best with both running side by side.
